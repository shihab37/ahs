About

Clear advice. Firm representation.

Syed Shah Zaib Hassan Naqvi leads AHS Advocates – Corporate & Tax Consultants. His practice centres on income tax and sales tax: registrations, returns, compliance with FBR and PRA, and appeals and references before the tax forums, along with legal drafting and representation.

Clients value his patient, straightforward approach. He listens carefully, explains every step in plain language, and keeps each matter on track until it is resolved.
